A Life in Many Acts

Some people spend their lives pursuing one passion. Stacylyn Bennett-Burnett has never been particularly good at limiting herself to just one. Actress, opera singer, violinist, mathematician at heart, and a woman whose life has taken more unexpected turns than any script she has ever performed, Stacylyn's journey has been anything but conventional.

From a three-year-old taking bows at a rodeo to a young violinist dreaming of the Utah Symphony, from an unexpected college scholarship to more than twenty-five years in New York City, her life has unfolded in a series of remarkable acts.

And while the stage has always been an important part of her world, some of her most interesting stories happened far beyond the footlights.
