THE PERFORMER

Professional Biography

Actress · Opera Singer · Performer

Stacylyn Bennett-Burnett is an American actress and classically trained soprano whose performing career has taken her from the opera houses and theaters of New York City to the worlds of independent film, television, and musical theater.

A graduate of the prestigious Manhattan School of Music, Stacylyn began her professional career in opera, where her rich lirico-spinto soprano voice and dramatic versatility brought her to some of the repertoire's most beloved and demanding roles. Her performances include Mimì in Puccini's La Bohème, Cio-Cio-San in Madama Butterfly, and Sister Angelica in Suor Angelica. She has also appeared as a featured concert soloist in New York, throughout the United States, and internationally.

Her passion for storytelling extends well beyond opera. On the theatrical stage, Stacylyn has portrayed characters ranging from Shakespeare's Juliet to the spirited Minnie in Ironworkers' Hayride, and has appeared in New York's Off-Broadway theater scene and the national tour of Terrence McNally's Tony Award-winning Master Class.

Transitioning into film and television, Stacylyn discovered another dimension of her love for acting. Her screen performances encompass an extraordinary range of characters, from vulnerable and compassionate women to deeply troubled, formidable, and delightfully comedic personalities. Her leading film credits include City of Salt, Ángel de la Muerte, Frigid Heart, and the award-winning short film Hey Brooklyn. Her television work includes appearances in Disney's Andi Mack and other productions.

Her performance in Hey Brooklyn drew particular praise from the Utah Film Festival, which recognized the emotional authenticity and compassion she brought to the role of Tina, a struggling single mother. Her work in City of Salt also earned recognition for Best Acting Performance in a Feature Film.

Throughout her career, Stacylyn has been drawn to the human experience behind every character—the humor, heartbreak, courage, vulnerability, and unexpected beauty that make each story worth telling.

Now residing in Cedar Hills, Utah, Stacylyn has created this website as both a personal retrospective and a celebration of the remarkable people, productions, and experiences that have shaped her artistic journey.

More than a collection of professional credits, these pages offer a glimpse behind the curtain: photographs, recordings, performances, and personal reflections from a lifetime devoted to the performing arts.

Welcome to her world of music, theater, and storytelling.
