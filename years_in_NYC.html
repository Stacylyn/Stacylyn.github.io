Two Years in New York

Her voice teacher was convinced that Stacylyn belonged in New York. Stacylyn wasn't quite so certain. Mathematics offered a practical career and a full scholarship. New York offered an uncertain future in an extraordinarily competitive profession.

So she made a deal.

She would go to New York City for two years and give herself a chance to pursue a singing career. If she couldn't make a go of it, she would return to Texas and accept the mathematics scholarship which would be waiting for her, but only for two years.

It seemed like a perfectly sensible compromise.

There was just one thing she hadn't anticipated.

She never went back to Texas.

Instead, those two experimental years became a professional performing career and more than twenty-five years of life in New York City.

The little girl who once stood up at a rodeo to accept applause had finally found her stage.
