My Journey with Master Class

Three years in New York. One extraordinary audition. A door that seemed ready to open.

The Audition

I had been in New York for only three years and had never performed in a New York theatrical production. But when the creative team heard me sing, something extraordinary happened.

The Opportunity

I was hired as a cover for the Broadway National Tour of Terrence McNally's Master Class, under the original Broadway director. I was the only non-Equity performer hired for the company, and the engagement was expected to make me immediately Equity-eligible.

The Unexpected Turn

Then a dispute involving American and British performers' unions and the casting of a British actress as Maria Callas changed everything. The Equity eligibility I had anticipated was rescinded.

It was a heartbreaking turn of events, but it could never erase what that audition had meant to me.
